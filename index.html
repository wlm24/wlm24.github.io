About

I am a third-year undergraduate in the Yao Class at Tsinghua University, the CS program founded by Turing Award laureate Andrew Chi-Chih Yao. I work on applied cryptography and the economics of autonomous-agent systems: payment-channel networks, Bitcoin protocol design, and payment infrastructure for LLM multi-agent systems.

Manuscripts Under Review

Colophon: Verifiable Agentic Payment to an Unknown Recipient Set

Liming Wang, Xiaoyu Fan, Wei Xu

Under review at USENIX Security 2027 · draft PDF

The first trustless payment protocol for a recipient set that only takes shape at runtime: before the payment becomes irrevocable, the payer obtains a cryptographic certificate of who was paid and how much, without trusting any intermediary. Realized on Bitcoin script extended with two near-future opcodes (CSFS, OP_CAT), at collateral independent of delegation depth and fan-out.

No API Key, No Problem: Enabling Agent-Centric Payments via Payment Channels

Liming Wang, Xiaoyu Fan, Wei Xu, Junda Liu

Under review at NeurIPS 2026 · draft PDF

Cost-aware payment middleware that replaces API-key billing in multi-agent systems with trust-minimized off-chain micropayments. Profiled 6,589 LLM calls across 270 tasks on three MAS frameworks and three backbone models; 0.6–1.4% runtime overhead and 35–250× lower settlement cost than on-chain baselines.
